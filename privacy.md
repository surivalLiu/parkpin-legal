# PlacePark — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 6, 2026  
**Contact:** [surival987@gmail.com](mailto:surival987@gmail.com)

This Privacy Policy explains how PlacePark (“we,” “us,” or “the App”) handles information when you use our iOS application. Please read it carefully. By using PlacePark, you acknowledge this Policy.

## 1. Who We Are

PlacePark is a consumer utility for **place-aware parking sessions**. It helps you:

- Save a parking location on your device with an optional note and photo
- Set a local timer reminder and navigate back using Apple Maps
- Configure **Place Rules** (for example, exclude Home/Work from auto-save; favorites with a default reminder duration)
- Optionally run **Save Parking** via Siri or the Shortcuts app (including automations such as CarPlay disconnect)

The App is designed to work primarily on your device without requiring an account.

## 2. Privacy Summary

- We do not create user accounts.
- We do not operate a PlacePark backend that receives your parking data.
- Parking sessions, place profiles, notes, photos, and reminders are stored on your device.
- We do not sell personal information.
- Optional one-time purchases are processed by Apple, not by a PlacePark server.

## 3. Information We Do Not Collect

PlacePark is designed so that we do not collect, transmit, or store on our servers: your name, email (except if you choose to email support), phone number, precise location history to us, parking photos, place profiles, payment card details, contacts, or advertising identifiers for tracking.

If App Store Connect privacy labels list “Data Not Collected,” that reflects this design. If we later add optional analytics or crash reporting, we will update this Policy and the App Privacy labels before doing so.

## 4. Information Stored on Your Device

To provide core features, the App may store locally on your iPhone or iPad:

- GPS coordinates and optional address text for parking sessions
- Optional notes (for example, floor or spot number) and optional photos you attach
- Reminder times, duration settings, and related preferences
- **Place profiles** you create (name, coordinates, radius, exclude vs favorite settings, optional default reminder)
- Match snapshots related to a session (for example, which place name applied when you saved)
- Purchase entitlement status restored via Apple

This information stays on your device unless you choose to share it yourself (for example, by taking a screenshot or opening Apple Maps). When you use Siri or Shortcuts to run Save Parking, the App may briefly use your current location on-device to apply Place Rules; that processing is not sent to a PlacePark server. Uninstalling the App typically deletes App-stored local data, subject to iOS behavior and any backups you create.

## 5. Device Permissions

PlacePark may request the following system permissions. You can deny or revoke them in iOS Settings; some features will then be unavailable.

- **Location (When In Use):** to save your parking spot, estimate distance back to your car, match Place Rules near you, and support Save Parking from Siri / Shortcuts. We do not use Background Location for continuous tracking.
- **Notifications:** to deliver local parking timer alerts and optional notices when auto-save is skipped by Place Rules.
- **Camera / Photo Library:** only if you choose to attach a photo of your parking spot or sign.

Permission prompts explain the purpose at the time of request.

## 6. Purchases and Apple Services

If you buy PlacePark Pro (or other in-app products), the transaction is processed by Apple through StoreKit / the App Store. We do not receive your full payment card number. Apple’s privacy terms apply to payment processing. The App may store a local flag that you have unlocked Pro, and may ask Apple to restore purchases.

When you tap Navigate, the App may open Apple Maps with coordinates you saved. Apple’s handling of map usage is governed by Apple’s policies. Siri and Shortcuts are Apple system features; their handling of voice and automation data is governed by Apple’s policies.

## 7. Children

PlacePark is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to us through support email, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

## 8. Retention and Deletion

Because parking and place data are stored on your device, you control them. You may delete place profiles and history entries in the App (where available), clear content by resetting the App’s data, or uninstall the App. Support emails you send to us are retained only as needed to respond and for legitimate business records, then deleted or minimized when no longer needed.

## 9. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain processing of personal data, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Because PlacePark does not maintain a cloud profile of your parking activity, many rights are exercised directly on your device (viewing, editing, or deleting local data; managing iOS permissions; restoring or managing purchases via Apple).

If you contact us about a privacy request, we may ask for information reasonably needed to verify and respond. We will not discriminate against you for exercising privacy rights.

## 10. International Users

PlacePark is offered globally through the App Store. On-device processing generally means your parking data is not transferred by us to our servers in another country. If you email support, your message may be received and processed where we operate support, which may be outside your country. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for such limited transfers.
