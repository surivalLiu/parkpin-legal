# ParkPin — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** July 17, 2026  
**Contact:** [surival987@gmail.com](mailto:surival987@gmail.com)

This Privacy Policy explains how ParkPin (“we,” “us,” or “the App”) handles information when you use our iOS application. Please read it carefully. By using ParkPin, you acknowledge this Policy.

## 1. Who We Are

ParkPin is a consumer utility that helps you save a parking location on your device, set a local timer reminder, and navigate back to your car using Apple Maps. The App is designed to work primarily on your device without requiring an account.

## 2. Privacy Summary

- We do not create user accounts.
- We do not operate a ParkPin backend that receives your parking data.
- Parking locations, notes, photos, and reminders are stored on your device.
- We do not sell personal information.
- Optional one-time purchases are processed by Apple, not by a ParkPin server.

## 3. Information We Do Not Collect

ParkPin is designed so that we do not collect, transmit, or store on our servers: your name, email (except if you choose to email support), phone number, precise location history to us, parking photos, payment card details, contacts, or advertising identifiers for tracking.

If App Store Connect privacy labels list “Data Not Collected,” that reflects this design. If we later add optional analytics or crash reporting, we will update this Policy and the App Privacy labels before doing so.

## 4. Information Stored on Your Device

To provide core features, the App may store locally on your iPhone or iPad:

- GPS coordinates and optional address text for your saved parking spot
- Optional notes (for example, floor or spot number)
- Optional photos you choose to attach
- Reminder times and related settings
- Purchase entitlement status restored via Apple

This information stays on your device unless you choose to share it yourself (for example, by taking a screenshot or opening Apple Maps). Uninstalling the App typically deletes App-stored local data, subject to iOS behavior and any backups you create.

## 5. Device Permissions

ParkPin may request the following system permissions. You can deny or revoke them in iOS Settings; some features will then be unavailable.

- **Location (When In Use):** to save your parking spot and estimate distance back to your car. We do not use Background Location for continuous tracking.
- **Notifications:** to deliver local parking timer alerts scheduled on your device.
- **Camera / Photo Library:** only if you choose to attach a photo of your parking spot or sign.

Permission prompts explain the purpose at the time of request.

## 6. Purchases and Apple Services

If you buy ParkPin Pro (or other in-app products), the transaction is processed by Apple through StoreKit / the App Store. We do not receive your full payment card number. Apple’s privacy terms apply to payment processing. The App may store a local flag that you have unlocked Pro, and may ask Apple to restore purchases.

When you tap Navigate, the App may open Apple Maps with coordinates you saved. Apple’s handling of map usage is governed by Apple’s policies.

## 7. Children

ParkPin is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to us through support email, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

## 8. Retention and Deletion

Because parking data is stored on your device, you control it. You may delete individual history entries in the App (where available), clear content by resetting the App’s data, or uninstall the App. Support emails you send to us are retained only as needed to respond and for legitimate business records, then deleted or minimized when no longer needed.

## 9. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain processing of personal data, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Because ParkPin does not maintain a cloud profile of your parking activity, many rights are exercised directly on your device (viewing, editing, or deleting local data; managing iOS permissions; restoring or managing purchases via Apple).

If you contact us about a privacy request, we may ask for information reasonably needed to verify and respond. We will not discriminate against you for exercising privacy rights.

## 10. International Users

ParkPin is offered globally through the App Store. On-device processing generally means your parking data is not transferred by us to our servers in another country. If you email support, your message may be received and processed where we operate support, which may be outside your country. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for such limited transfers.

## 11. U.S. State Privacy Notices (including California)

If you are a California resident (or resident of another U.S. state with similar laws such as the CCPA/CPRA and comparable statutes), this section supplements the Policy.

- Categories collected by us via the App: generally none for cloud storage; local device storage is described above. Support email content you voluntarily send may include identifiers and message content.
- We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.
- We do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for purposes that require a right to limit under CPRA, beyond what is necessary to provide the App or respond to you.
- You may request to know, delete, or correct personal information we hold about you (typically limited to support correspondence). Submit requests to the contact email below.
- We do not knowingly sell or share personal information of consumers under 16.

## 12. EEA, UK, and Similar Regions

If GDPR, UK GDPR, or similar laws apply, note that ParkPin’s core parking features process data on your device under your control. We generally do not act as a cloud “controller” of your parking coordinates. Where we process support emails, our legal bases may include: taking steps at your request before a contract, performance of a contract, legitimate interests in responding to inquiries and securing our communications, or consent where required.

You may have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and data portability, and the right to withdraw consent where processing is consent-based. You may also contact your local data protection authority.
